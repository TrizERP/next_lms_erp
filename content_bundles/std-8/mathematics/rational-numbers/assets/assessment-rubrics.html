Section 1

Overview

Each section below rubrics one property across four bands – Excellent, Good, Fair, Needs Improvement – so a mark reflects genuine understanding of why a property holds or fails, not just a correctly computed answer. Each section closes with sample questions new to this document.

Section 10

A Worked Full-Mark Answer

Example 3 from the chapter (abbreviated): find 2/5 × (−3/7) − 1/14 − 3/7 × 3/5. A full-mark answer groups the two terms sharing −3/7: (−3/7) × (2/5 + 3/5) − 1/14 = (−3/7) × 1 − 1/14 = −3/7 − 1/14 = −7/14 = −1/2. The mark-scheme reward is for recognising the shared factor, not merely reaching the final number.

Marking noteAward method marks for identifying −3/7 as the common factor even if the final arithmetic has a slip – the strategic insight is the harder skill being assessed here.
